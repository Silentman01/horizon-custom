## Angles and mechanisms
- Pain: dry heels / stubborn callus; enemy = pumice stones, manual files, salon pedicures ("zonder raspen of dure pedicures"). Promise: smooth feet "in 5 minutes", painless, safe at home, 100-day guarantee. Social proof: Nuvé "#1 rated ... loved by 100,000+ customers"; LUNÉ "10.000+ vrouwen/klanten". Mechanism is thin: "Clinically proven", "Silk" (soft finish); no real explained mechanism in copy (rotating sanding roller; not transcribed).

## Concentration, moat, sophistication
- Nuvé dominates (~$8.5k/day vs LUNÉ $1.3k; almost all of the market's visible spend). Moat is brand scale, 1,000 live ads, 100k+ customer claim, not a patent; the product is a commodity white-label rotary file (many clones at EUR 20-45). LUNÉ is the one successful localiser (NL).
- Schwartz: stage 3-4. The basic claim "smooth feet in minutes" is saturated since at least Apr 2025 and every clone repeats it; buyers know electric callus removers. Differentiation now needs new mechanism/avatar (gifting, men's feet, seniors, diabetic-adjacent care claims are regulated) rather than the base promise.
