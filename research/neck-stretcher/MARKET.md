## Answer to the key question
Not thin. Neck traction is a real, multi-year category with spend in three separate places, but the two starting cards are one small old market (myspinea) and one brand-new clone wave (monsori). The biggest 3-in-1 (heat + massage + inflatable stretch) market is not monsori, it is `doctinolab.fr` (Cervéa), a one-seller market that sits outside monsori's market card.

## Angles and mechanisms
1. Proven testimonial template: "I had neck pain 2+ years, tried everything, after 9 days it was gone" + five stars + "no pills, no expensive chiropractor/treatments". Originated at azouna.com in German (ad 1809310289998122, "Anna L, DE", running 2025-08-01 to 2026-11-13, about 469 days, EU reach 129,394, FB post 623 reactions/144 comments). The identical text, with "Martin G., de Bordeaux", runs at doctinolab (ads 1726414191754664: reach 938,333, EUR 7,731 spend; 1801338641065285: reach 808,219, EUR 6,671) and at cocoony (1685272176446633, 2585371855209587, 1453280719575678). So the wave copies the German winner.
2. Mechanism claim (doctinolab 1981260692569882): "Étirement Airlift® à 39°" (traction), "Chaleur profonde thérapeutique à 42°" (does not cool after 30 min like heating pads), "Massage par impulsions profondes"; "premier coussin de kiné à domicile au monde"; "start-up française", "analysé les retours de plus de 106 400 clients et, avec des kinésithérapeutes".
3. Myspinea (long-run advertorial funnel, lp): occipital nerve compression "que les médecins ignorent", "Dr Laurent", women 40+, 15 min before sleep, "Plus de 12 000 femmes" (ad 1856691495734713); video testimonial versions (26904667175786208, 1606072927299098, 1278037994476754) all started 2026-03-22 (about 230+ days).
4. Callixe: clinician/creator voice "muscles + spinal compression + irritated nerves", traction + heat + vibration; IG creator posts (831544826660042: 686 likes, 65 comments, 384,689 views).
5. Hyperblade: posture / range of motion testimonial ("couldn't check my blind spot").

## Concentration and moat
Within the FR/NL 3-in-1 wave, doctinolab holds about $2,500 of roughly $3,300 combined daily spend of monsori + doctinolab (~75%). No moat found: product is white-label/commodity, stores 2-4 weeks old, no patents or reviews edge visible; moat = speed and creative volume. In English traction pillows, Callixe leads by ad count; creator-driven.

## Market sophistication (Schwartz)
Stage 3-4 in EU French/Dutch: buyers know "neck stretcher"; ads compete on mechanism (Airlift 39°, heat 42°, pulse massage), authority (kiné, "Dr Laurent", "cliniquement confirmé") and numbers (106,400 / 107,000+ customers). The pure claim "relieves neck pain" is not enough; a stage-3 mechanism and stage-4 proof (specific nerve/decompression explanation, guarantees) is the norm. German/Italian/Spanish/Nordic markets are earlier (stage 2-3).

## Sourcing and margin
Brandsearch sourcing match exists only on myspinea: $5.01 unit, ~$12 shipping, 94.8% gross margin before fees, break-even ROAS 1.26, CPA $76.96. CAUTION: the matched 1688 item is a heated memory-foam "non-traction" cervical pillow, not the inflatable stretcher, so this is an approximate floor, not a quote. Retail $70-90 gives high markup in any case. Shipping complexity (my read, not from the data): small and light, but contains a battery and heating element (EU CE/EMC/battery compliance, returns, "clinical" claims risk).

## Gaps
- Unserved or thinly served language markets for the heat + massage 3-in-1: DE (only azouna declining 48 active, Cervafix/Calmory inactive), IT (frescali 0 active), Spain (only MX rorilio, 5 ads), PL, Nordics (nordcura 2 active, svendy 0), UK (morieva 0 active).
- US: English traction pillow (Callixe $129) and manual $179-199 devices dominate; no heated 3-in-1 at ~$75-90 with a large active ad base appears in the data (but spend there is not measurable).
- Avatar: wave targets 45-64 (monsori 55-64 and 65+ = 62%, men 65.8%); doctinolab/myspinea skew female 63-65%, 45-54. Younger desk workers (25-44, about 33-37% of myspinea reach) are barely addressed.
- Angle unused: sleep/morning-stiffness (monsori pillow ads use it for a different product), desk-worker/gamer, sciatic/migraine.

## One-line read
There is room, but only as a fast, differentiated entry into a language market the wave has not reached (DE/IT/ES/PL/Nordics, or UK) or with a new mechanism; FR/NL is already flooded by a copy of a 15-month-old German winner within the last 30 days.
